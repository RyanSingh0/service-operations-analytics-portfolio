# Findings from the May 8, 2016 snapshot

These findings were checked against normalized source observations and the incident snapshot, not inferred from dashboard appearance. They describe a historical extract and do not establish operational causes.

1. **Group 9 is a cutoff-dependent concentration of open work.** There are 215 incidents assigned to this group at the cutoff: 106 Active, 104 New, two Awaiting User Info, and three Closed. Therefore 212 are open (98.6%). Ninety were opened in March, 105 in April, and 20 in May. Open incidents have a median age of about 804.7 hours (33.5 days). In a separate retrospective follow-up using later observations, all 215 become terminal by the end of the extract, and only 68 still have Group 9 as their latest assignment. Later evidence is used here to investigate the finding; it is not inserted into May 8 metrics. The extract does not identify the group's function, staffing, assignment policy, or cause of delay.

2. **Unknown is missing ownership, mostly on terminal records.** Among 2,203 incidents with a missing latest assignment group, 2,102 are Closed and 59 Resolved; 42 remain open. A nonzero recorded reassignment count appears on 1,834 (83.2%). The first visible observation has a named group for 1,797 (81.6%); 1,425 initially belonged to Group 70. This supports interpreting Unknown as a data-completeness issue in current ownership, not as a real team performing 1,834 reassignments. The log does not establish why these values are absent, and the counter is not a count of observed distinct groups.

3. **Resolution is strongly right-skewed and excludes open work.** The median observed closing-cycle duration is 12.08 hours and p90 is 292.12 hours, about 24 times the median. The 1,262 open incidents are excluded from these duration summaries. Ranking groups by their resolved-only median would mix incomplete follow-up, case mix, and current ownership; it would not be a fair causal efficiency comparison. The separate survival study retains observable right-censored cases and uses a different first-resolution target.

4. **Small rejection counts do not mean perfect data.** Five of 105,372 consumed deliveries are quarantined for update timestamps before opening. Required-schema, identity, and integrity checks pass, but missing categories and sparse coverage remain meaningful limitations. The much later sparse tail of the full extract is unsuitable for a confident year-long arrival forecast. NYC 311 forecasting therefore uses a separate current source and is not mixed into the IT incident totals.
